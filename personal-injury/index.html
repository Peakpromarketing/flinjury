Types of Personal Injury Cases We Handle

Car Accidents

Whether caused by distracted driving, speeding, or failure to obey traffic laws, car crashes can leave victims with serious injuries. Even if you were partially at fault, Florida’s comparative negligence laws may still entitle you to compensation. John can help determine fault and fight for your share of the recovery.

Motorcycle Accidents

Motorcyclists are especially vulnerable on the road. If you’ve been injured by a negligent driver, we’ll work to hold them accountable. Attorney Andreopoulos builds strong cases to pursue maximum compensation for your injuries, medical expenses, and lost income.

Truck Accidents

Collisions involving large trucks can be catastrophic. We investigate the cause — whether driver fatigue, overloaded cargo, or negligent maintenance — and pursue claims against all responsible parties, including trucking companies and their insurers.

Slip and Fall Accidents

Property owners are responsible for keeping their premises safe. If you slipped due to a wet floor, uneven surface, or poor lighting, you may be eligible for compensation. Florida law requires fast action — John will begin investigating right away to preserve evidence.

Wrongful Death

If you’ve lost a loved one due to someone’s negligent or reckless actions, we can help you pursue a wrongful death claim. You may be entitled to damages for both economic losses (like lost wages) and emotional suffering. While no amount of money can bring back a loved one, fair compensation can ease financial burdens and bring some measure of closure.

Negligent Security

Victims of assault, robbery, or other violent crimes may have a claim if a property owner failed to provide adequate security. Whether it’s a broken lock, no cameras, or untrained security staff, John will help determine if negligent security played a role in your injury.

What to Do If You’ve Been Injured

If you or a loved one has been hurt, it’s critical to act quickly. Here’s what we recommend:

  • Don’t sign any forms from insurance companies before speaking with us
  • Save all medical bills, pay stubs, and accident documentation
  • Contact us immediately for a free consultation
